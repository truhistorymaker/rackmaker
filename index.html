RackMaker

Conduit bending calculator for iPhone

Support

Questions, a number that does not look right, or something you would like added? A support email address will be listed here shortly.

It helps to say which bend you were on, the conduit size, and the numbers you entered. The Share button on any bend puts all of that into a message for you.

  • The marks look off for my bender. Open Setup and enter the deduct, gain and centerline radius stamped on your own bender for that conduit size. The starting numbers are for a standard hand bender.
  • The angle level reads strangely. Tap Zero with the phone held on the straight pipe or the bender handle before each pull, and keep the phone on the same spot. It reads tilt, so the bend has to travel up or down.
  • Start a bend over. Tap Reset all under the bend, then tap it again.

Always bend a test piece before a production run.

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026.

RackMaker does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. The app makes no network connections.

  • The measurements, settings and saved racks you enter are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • The angle level reads your phone's motion sensor to show the bend angle. Those readings are used on the phone at that moment and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • When you tap Share, the bend is handed to the app you pick (Messages, Mail, a printer and so on). What happens to it after that is up to that app.

If you email for support, your email address and what you write are used only to answer you.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions about it can go to the support email above.
